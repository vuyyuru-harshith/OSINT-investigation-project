# OSINT Investigation Methodology

## Objective

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how publicly available
information can be collected, verified, organized and analyzed to develop
a structured intelligence profile of a company.

## Investigation Scope

The investigation focuses on:

1. Company information
2. Leadership and organizational information
3. Digital presence
4. Domain information
5. Technology footprint
6. News and media coverage
7. Competitors
8. Major events and timeline
9. Publicly available reports
10. Source verification

## Data Sources

Only publicly available information is used.

Examples include:

- Official company website
- Annual reports
- Government/public regulatory sources
- Reputable news organizations
- Public social media profiles
- Public domain information
- Public technology information

## Ethical Guidelines

This project does not involve:

- Unauthorized access
- Password collection
- Credential harvesting
- Private information
- Stalking or tracking individuals
- Exploitation of systems
- Bypassing authentication
- Non-public data collection

The project is intended strictly for educational and ethical OSINT research.
